# Privacy Policy for Cozy Village Survival

**Effective date:** October 8, 2026

This policy explains what information the game **Cozy Village Survival** (the "game") uses, where it is kept, and how you can delete it. The game is made by **ItsElton** ("we", "us").

## The short version

- We don't run any servers, and we never receive your information.
- The game has no ads, no in-app purchases, and no analytics or tracking of our own.
- Your island is saved on your phone. If you choose to, you can also keep a copy in your own Google account through Google Play Games, where only the game, signed in to your account, can read it.

## Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device so you can carry on where you left off. This includes your island, your villagers, your level and quests, the **player name** you type in, and the avatar you pick. It also stores your settings, such as sound and music. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

## Google Play Games (Android)

On Android, the game uses **Google Play Games Services**, a service provided by Google.

- **Signing in.** If you already have a Google Play Games profile, Google may sign you in to the game automatically when it starts. You can also sign in from the game's Settings. Google then shares your Play Games **gamer name, avatar and player ID** with the game. The game uses these only to show that you are signed in and to keep your cloud save linked to your account.
- **Cloud save (optional).** If you turn on cloud save, a copy of your saved game is stored using Google Play Games **Saved Games**. That copy includes your progress and your player name, and it lives in a private app-data area of your Google account's Google Drive. It is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). We cannot see it or access it. It is used only so you can get your island back on a new phone or after reinstalling the game.
- **Information Google collects.** The Google Play Games software in the game collects some information for Google, such as diagnostic and usage information, to run and improve its services. That information goes to Google, not to us, and Google's Privacy Policy applies to it: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Android backup

Android can back up app data to your Google account and restore it when you reinstall the game or set up a new phone. If you use this feature, your saved island and settings may be included. Android and Google run this backup, not us, and you can switch it on or off in your device's settings.

## Sharing

We don't sell, share or transfer your information to anyone. The only services involved are Google's (Google Play Games and Android backup), as described above.

## Keeping and deleting your information

- **On your device:** uninstalling the game deletes its saved data from your device.
- **Cloud save:** you can delete the game's cloud save and other Play Games data for this game from your Google Play Games profile settings. These are in the Google Play Store app, or at https://play.google.com/games/profile, under the option to delete your data for an individual game. Deleting your whole Play Games profile removes this data for all games.
- **Android backup:** you can manage or delete device backups in your device's Google backup settings.

Because we never receive your information, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. If you have a question, contact us at the address below.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and change the effective date above.
