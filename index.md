# Cozy Village Survival

**Cozy Village Survival** is a relaxing island village game for Android phones, made by **ItsElton**.

You start on a small island with one villager and a campfire. Tap trees, rocks, berry bushes and wild boars, and your villagers walk over to gather wood, stone and food. Build and upgrade tents, invite new villagers, repair the old fishing dock, and complete quests to level up and unlock more of the island. Days turn into nights and the weather changes, but there's no rush: your villagers never die, and you can play at your own pace.

Your island is saved on your phone. If you sign in with Google Play Games, you can also keep a backup in the cloud and get your island back on a new phone.
